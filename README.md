## Combat Campus

A Brazilian jiu-jitsu competition archive built for athletes and gyms. Developed through conversations with athletes and gym owners and refined using their feedback.

[Explore Combat Campus](https://combatcampus.base44.app/)

## Referral time calculator

An independent concept built around a possible go-to-market use case for Exacare: helping care teams explore how changes in referral review time could affect their workload. The calculator uses example figures and visitor-entered assumptions; its output is illustrative, not a prediction or guarantee. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Exacare.

[Try the calculator](https://referral-time-estimator-julien.divineskink9.chatgpt.site/) · [View the source](referral-time-calculator/index.html)
